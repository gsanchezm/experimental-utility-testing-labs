# E10-visual executor -- DRAFT (operation OP-PREP-E10)

**DRAFT. Not frozen, not locked, not dispatched.** Nothing in this directory starts, configures, authorizes, or dispatches a campaign. Its measured workflow fails closed until the E10 campaign START, the locked "Campaign configuration" record, the calibration record of the thresholds, a dispatch authorization of the human research lead (protocol/agent-governance-v6.md, Section 3.7, FROZEN-PRE-DATA since 2026-11-06), a controlled-instance verification on GitHub Actions, the frozen dispatch prompt and the toolchain entry of the instrument are all present. Working basis: experiments/E10-visual/design-brief-draft.md and campaign-configuration-draft.md; the conditions are in conditions-draft.json. Deviations and open items: IMPLEMENTATION-NOTES.md (same folder).

## 1. What one execution does, and what it never does

For one condition the job runs N = 50 executions one after the other on one shared instance (nothing is restarted). One execution is: the **starting-state check** (SUT-01: the web surface and the API health answer 200 and both accounts log in through `POST /api/auth/login` with the behavior the condition declares; SUT-02 and SUT-03: the surface answers 200); the **method**, for each view of the condition in order, three captures in the fixed order A, B, A2 (State A, the perturbed State B, State A again), each in a **fresh browser context** (headless Chromium, viewport 1280 x 900, device scale factor 1, time zone UTC, locale en-US, reduced motion) with its own activation of the account, followed by the two comparisons A against B and A against A2; the **classification** from the comparisons by the rule of the condition; the **record** (bundle, run manifest last).

Per capture the executor activates the account (SUT-01: the client state of the E03 SUT-01 web realization seeded on a first load of the web root; SUT-02: the login form of the application; SUT-03 IMAGE: the sign-in page of the application; SUT-03 SHIFT: the login page of the performance test area), calls `run.mark_stimulus_sent()` immediately before the first **measured navigation** of the execution (the boundary of attribution), navigates to the view's route with `wait_until='commit'`, runs the view's steps (SUT-02 `menu_open`: a click on the burger icon, then waits for the close icon), waits for the view's ready signal (bounded), for a timed view waits until the document clock (`performance.now()`, origin: the start of the navigation) reaches the target time, runs the settle check, reads the boxes of the expected elements and the state of their images, and takes one viewport screenshot at scroll position 0 (animations disabled, caret hidden). It never performs a Required Action (`actions` is absent), never retries, never intercepts or answers a request of the page, never writes under `raw-data/` outside a measured workflow run, and never reads another campaign's or another SUT's output.

## 2. Conditions (candidate ids, not frozen until the configuration is locked)

| Condition id | SUT | State A | State B | Views | Expected elements |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E10-SUT01-WEB-PROBLEM | SUT-01 | `standard_user` | `problem_user` | `catalog` (`/catalog`) | price and image of every item wholly inside the viewport in A |
| E10-SUT02-WEB-VISUAL | SUT-02 | `standard_user` | `visual_user` | `inventory` (`/inventory.html`), `cart` (`/cart.html`), `menu_open` (`/inventory.html` after a click on the burger icon) | cart icon container and burger icon; checkout button; close icon |
| E10-SUT03-WEB-IMAGE | SUT-03 | `demouser` | `image_not_loading_user` | `shelf` (`/`) | every product thumbnail wholly inside the viewport in A |
| E10-SUT03-WEB-SHIFT | SUT-03 | `testuser` at 2000 ms | `testuser` at 5500 ms | `high_cls` (`/sut/ui/high-cls`) | `#shift-target` |

The rule (campaign-configuration-draft.md, section 3; classify.py): per view `R` is the differing pixels of A against B over the compared pixels; an expected element is *considered* when its box is wholly inside the viewport in A; its region is the union of its box in A and in B clipped to the capture; `r(e)` is the differing pixels inside the region over the pixels of the region; the view is detected when `R >= T_R` and `r(e) >= T_e` for every element considered (and every expected group has at least one); the execution is `DETECTED` when every view is. The false-positive record of a view is `R` of A against A2 `>= T_R` (the box clause is not applied). `T_R` and `T_e` come from the calibration record (`classify.calibrate`: floors 0.0005 and 0.01, factor 2, baseline-against-baseline pairs only); they are `null` in the draft, and with `null` a DRY_RUN or DEVELOPMENT run uses the floors and labels every result provisional (`thresholds.provisional` in the detection record), while a MEASURED_EXPERIMENT is refused.

## 3. Outcome categories and the run status

| outcome_category | status | matches_expected | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| DETECTED | SUCCESS | true | the rule holds in every view, every required ready signal present |
| NOT_DETECTED | FAILURE | false | valid measurements, the rule does not hold (a recorded non-detection, not an error) |
| INCONCLUSIVE_READY_SIGNAL_MISSING | INCONCLUSIVE | null | A or B never showed the page's own content, a timed capture was taken too late, or a group of expected elements has no element wholly inside the viewport in A |
| INCONCLUSIVE_CAPTURE_DIMENSION_MISMATCH | INCONCLUSIVE | null | a capture is not 1280 x 900, or A and B differ in size (a harness defect, never a non-detection) |
| INCONCLUSIVE_EXTERNAL_IMAGE_NOT_LOADED | INCONCLUSIVE | null | SUT-01 only: an expected image of State A (in A, or in A2 when A2 is valid) did not load; attribution EXTERNAL |
| START_SURFACE_NOT_READY, START_ACTIVATION_NOT_CONFIRMED | ABORTED | null | a surface did not answer, or an account's activation (behavior, cookie, sign-in) was not confirmed |
| INTERRUPTED_ | ERROR | null | an exception of the harness or of the client, the comparison instrument failing, or the watchdog |
| STUB_NO_BUNDLE | ERROR | null | the bundle of an execution could not be completed; a stub is written |

Attribution is mechanical (E05 D-16): TOOLING for an exception before the first measured navigation, SUT otherwise and when uncertain, EXTERNAL for the one external-image category. The per-execution detail (thresholds used and whether provisional, per view `R`, `r(e)` of each element with its boxes, the unlisted-difference ratio, the false-positive record) is `evidence/NNN-detection.json` and the per-comparison records `evidence/NNN-<view>-AB-comparison.json` and `-AA2-comparison.json`, so every number can be recomputed from the evidence.

## 4. Instance, environment, and bundle

- `instance.json` (interface version 1; the layout of experiments/E03-resetability/executor/README.md, section 6): surfaces `web` at `http://127.0.0.1:3000` (SUT-01 also `api` at `http://127.0.0.1:8000`), the provisioning record (repository, commit, route), `process` (SUT-01 only, recorded and never used for a restart).
- Bundle (`executions/<run_id>/` of the job's artifact; a measured run is imported as `raw-data/E10-visual/<SUT>/<run_id>/`, the path its run manifest lists; the artifact root holds the job-level files): `run-manifest.json` (written last), `executor.log`, `attempt-record.json`, `tool-versions.json`, `ci-provenance.json`, `runtime-environment.json`, and `evidence/` with one numbered file per record: `instance`, `instrument` (capture version, the Pixelmatch pin as used, its options, the Node.js version, the runner digest, the thresholds), the login transcripts of SUT-01 (passwords redacted), `activation`, per capture `<view>-<role>-capture.png` and `-capture.json` (boxes, image states, settle result, document clock), `-activation`, `-network-log`, `-console-log`, per comparison `<view>-AB-diff.png` and `-comparison.json` (and `-AA2-`), and `detection`.
- The scrubber replaces the password of each account of SUT-01 and SUT-02, every access token, host paths, and private network addresses before any text file is kept. The documented dummy passwords of SUT-03 (`password` of the test area, `testingisfun99` of the sign-in page, both shown by the pages themselves) are not registered as secrets. PNG files are written as the browser produced them and the diff PNGs as the comparison produced them.

## 5. Run manifest (schemas/run-manifest.schema.v2.json)

`campaign_id` E10-visual; `experiment_id` and `condition` the condition id; `sut_id`; `platform` web; `modality` "Visual Testing"; `attempt`; `outcome` {status, expected, observed, matches_expected, outcome_category}; `behavior_class` the condition's (external dependency for SUT-01, deterministic for the others); `reset_mechanisms`, `establishment_mechanisms`, `target_state_id` and `starting_state_id` null; `seed` null; no `actions`, no `scenario`; `agent_role` ORCHESTRATOR; `prompt_version` the frozen executor prompt id; `protocol_version` v1; `toolchain` (web_runner, browser, visual, comparison_runtime, executor_runtime, scripting_runtime, and api_client for SUT-01, each with a version observed in the run; the Pixelmatch tarball and module digests and the capture version are in `notes` and in `evidence/instrument.json`, because the schema allows only `tool` and `version` per toolchain entry); `sut_provenance` (the components with repository and commit). tests/test_attempt.py checks the manifests against the schema subset.

## 6. Implementation integrity (the E03 lock mechanism, with its own digests)

`run-condition.sh` embeds the sha256 of every `executor/executor/*.py` file and of `requirements.txt`, verifies them, and refuses any unlisted Python file under `executor/executor/`, before anything else. The digests are computed by `tests/embed_digests.py` (run it last, after every edit of an executor file); they are not typed. `tests/test_digests.py` re-checks them independently. The Node.js runner of the comparison is the constant `NODE_RUNNER_JS` of `executor/compare.py` (so it is under the lock; no `.mjs` file lives in `executor/executor/`). The gate of the measured workflow locks the single hash of `run-condition.sh`, which covers the whole implementation, and checks that `tools.visual` of the toolchain manifest equals the pins of the implementation (the lines `PIXELMATCH_VERSION`, `PIXELMATCH_TARBALL_SHA256` and `PIXELMATCH_INDEX_SHA256` of compare.py, `CAPTURE_VERSION` of capture.py, and the Playwright pin of requirements.txt). The Pixelmatch tarball is downloaded by the executor itself (one attempt, no retry), verified by sha256, registry sha512 integrity and sha1 shasum, and only the member `package/index.js` is read, in memory, verified by sha256 and written as `pixelmatch.mjs` into a temporary directory; a mismatch refuses the run before any record is written (exit status 2). The environment variable `E10_PIXELMATCH_TARBALL` names a local copy of the same tarball for tests and development; the same digests apply.

The future entry `tools.visual` of manifests/toolchain-manifest.yaml (written only after the human research lead approves it, never by this draft) has the fields: `candidate`, `version` (`8.0.0`, the Pixelmatch pin), `playwright_version` (`1.63.0`), `capture_version` (`e10-capture-v1`), `tarball_sha256`, `index_sha256`, `browser`, `pinned_by`, `pinned_at`, `install_date` (null), `platform` (null), `notes`.

## 7. Tests (static; run on any Python 3 with Node.js for the script and runner tests)

Run from `tests/`: `python3 -m unittest discover -s . -p 'test_*.py' -v` (set `PYTHONPATH` to a PyYAML copy when the interpreter has none; set `E10_PIXELMATCH_TARBALL` to a local copy of the registry tarball to run the cases of the real module).

| File | What it checks |
|---|---|
| test_classify.py | the geometry, the rule at one pixel below, at and above each threshold, the per-view conjunction, the union of the boxes of A and B, the zero-area box, the elements not wholly inside the viewport, the false-positive record, the inconclusive categories, the external-image rule, calibration (all-zero baselines give the floors), attribution and the manifest outcome |
| test_compare.py | the PNG codec against a reference encoder (RGB and RGBA, the five filter types), the pin (good tarball, wrong tarball digest, wrong member digest, wrong integrity or shasum, absent or non-regular member, extra members ignored), the local-tarball hook, the Node.js runner with a stub module (identical images, one changed block, mask and diff image, dimension mismatch, a failing runner, a mask that disagrees with the count) and, when `E10_PIXELMATCH_TARBALL` is set, with the real pinned module |
| test_capture.py | the setup values, the selectors, the timing check, the order of the calls of one capture with a fake page (timed and untimed, not ready), the steps, every in-page script (syntax in Node.js and behaviour against a mock of the DOM) |
| test_config.py | the conditions file and its validation (one mutation per refusal), the thresholds (a pair per condition, null in the draft; a pair in `rule` is refused) |
| test_attempt.py | the attempt loop with a fake realization (no browser, no Node.js): categories, attribution, bundles, comparison and detection records, manifests against the schema subset, no secret in any file, the stub path |
| test_run.py | the entry checks of `executor.run` (a measured run needs the workflow and calibrated thresholds) and the registry of realizations |
| test_digests.py, test_run_condition.py | the embedded digests; a tampered or unlisted file is refused by the entry point |

Not tested here: the behaviour of the browser (no Chromium in the test environment), the rendering of the pages and their images, the speed of the PNG decoding on real screenshots, and the live SUTs. The first real evidence of these is an ENVIRONMENT_VERIFICATION DRY_RUN (section 9). The tests of the gate and of the workflows (test_gates.py, test_workflows.py) belong to the workflow files and are described with them.

## 8. Dispatch authorization record (governance v6, Section 3.7) -- the fields the gate reads

The human research lead issues the record; no agent writes it as an authorization. The gate reads `manifests/e10-dispatch-authorization-sut0N-v*.yaml` (class `E10_DISPATCH_AUTHORIZATION`) and matches `authorization_id` to the dispatch input. Required fields: `authorization_id`, `authorization_date`, `issued_by` (`human research lead`), `status` (ISSUED), `record_validity` (VALID), `dispatch_eligibility` (ELIGIBLE), `revoked` (false), `assigned_sut`, `conditions` (the executable conditions of that SUT), `n_executions` (50), `attempt` (1), `campaign_start_authorization_id`, and `frozen_inputs` with `executor_implementation_sha256` and `executor_prompt_sha256` (the SHA-256 of the frozen prompt file). A further attempt needs a record `manifests/e10-further-attempt-approval-sut0N-v*.yaml` of class `E10_FURTHER_ATTEMPT_APPROVAL` (gate G-E10-A2). The thresholds `T_R` and `T_e` of each condition are inputs of the locked configuration (the calibration record); the executor refuses a measured run in which they are null.

## 9. Environment verification and DRY_RUN

`.github/workflows/e10-environment-verification.yml` (dispatch-only, manual) provisions the condition's SUT from the pinned commit on a GitHub-hosted runner, writes `instance.json` with record class DRY_RUN, and runs this executor for two or three executions per condition (never fifty). Its bundles are labeled DRY_RUN and are never imported, never measured and never a source for an expectation; their baseline-against-baseline pairs (A against A2) are the only input of the calibration rule. The plan of the rounds is experiments/E10-visual/dry-run-plan-draft.md.
