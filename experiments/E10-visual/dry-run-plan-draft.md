# E10-visual: DRY_RUN plan (DRAFT-1, 2026-11-28 UTC)

**DRAFT. Nothing here dispatches, starts, authorizes or freezes anything.** A DRY_RUN is a non-measured verification: its bundles are reviewed, never imported into raw-data/ and never counted. Workflow: `.github/workflows/e10-environment-verification.yml` (dispatch-only). Executor: experiments/E10-visual/executor/. Conditions: conditions-draft.json (four conditions; SUT-02 has three views). Working basis: campaign-configuration-draft.md (sections 3, 4 and 8).

## 1. What must be public first

GitHub Actions evaluates only the public repository (the agent-instructions file, "E03 CI/CD infrastructure"). The two workflows, the executor, `conditions-draft.json`, the working prompt `prompts/working/ORCHESTRATOR-E10-visual-v1.md` and the design drafts reach the public repository only through a public export (the next one after the last logged in PUBLIC-EXPORT-MANIFEST.yaml; the refresh D31 of the status page travels in it). Each export push needs the human research lead's yes to its exact line. The first export that carries a new workflow file also shows whether GitHub accepts it (the validation workflow checks the run-script length limit of OP-CI-15).

## 3. What round 1 must show (open items of campaign-configuration-draft.md, sections 4, 6 and 8)

1. **The first screen of each page and the elements wholly inside it** (V-1, V-3, V-4). For every view: which elements named in the expectation lie wholly inside the 1280 x 900 viewport in State A (SUT-01: how many price and image boxes of the catalogue; SUT-02: whether the checkout button of the cart page and the close icon of the open drawer lie inside; SUT-03: how many thumbnails of the shelf), that the page showed its own content (the ready signals), and that State B shows the same elements.
2. **What an empty `src` and a failed external image render as** (V-1). SUT-03: what Chromium paints for `image_not_loading_user`. SUT-01: whether the 12 images of State A (`upload.wikimedia.org`) loaded from the runner, what the failing host `broken-image-url.com` did (name resolution, time to failure), and whether the `onError` placeholder of State B loaded; the recorded natural width of every expected image, per capture. If the host refuses runner traffic, the condition is recorded `INCONCLUSIVE_EXTERNAL_IMAGE_NOT_LOADED` in the affected executions and the human research lead decides what that means; the expectation is not changed to fit.
3. **The baseline-against-baseline ratios** (V-2): `R` and `r(e)` of A against A2, per view and per expected element, over every execution of the round. These are the only inputs of the calibration of `T_R` and `T_e` (campaign-configuration-draft.md, section 3, calibration rule): the thresholds are calibrated from the baseline pairs of the rounds only, never from A against B. The round also shows whether any baseline pair exceeds the floors (0.0005 and 0.01); a noisy pair is not a reason to run again: it raises the thresholds by the formula (twice the largest baseline ratio), and the rule is not adjusted to the perturbed ratios. Until a calibration record exists, a DRY_RUN classifies with the floors and labels the classification provisional.
4. **SUT-02 perturbation visibility and unlisted differences** (V-3): that the moved cart icon container, the rotated burger and close icons and the repositioned checkout button are visible at the chosen viewport; the differences outside the expectation (random prices, the first item's image, the aligned items, the user name), recorded as unlisted differences and never counted as a detection.
5. **Shelf settle and the 2000 / 5500 ms captures** (V-4): that the SUT-03 shelf is stable at the capture (the products are fetched after mount), that the three captures of `/sut/ui/high-cls` at 2000, 5500 and 2000 ms after the document commit are taken at those times (the recorded `performance.now()` at each capture), that `#shift-target` has no area in State A and its box in State B, and whether `#target-text` moves (recorded, not chosen).
6. **PNG sizes per execution** (section 6 of the configuration draft; decision E10-D-20): the size of each capture and diff image, of an execution and of a projected 50 executions of each condition, against the repository and export limits. No image is dropped in a DRY_RUN either.
7. **The durations of an execution of each condition**, from `duration_s` in the attempt records (decision E10-D-19: the measured job limit and the executor watchdog are set from them).
8. **The Node.js that runs the comparison**: the same major (24) at the executor step of the three job groups (SUT-01, SUT-02 and SUT-03, where the build uses Node.js 14 and Node.js 24 is put first on the path again), and the minimum major of 20 that the Node.js step enforces.
9. **The version strings** of Playwright, Chromium and Node.js in the run manifests and in `runtime-environment.json` (`comparison_runtime`), and the digests of the Pixelmatch tarball and module the executor verified, for the entry `tools.visual` of manifests/toolchain-manifest.yaml (decision E10-D-13).

## 4. After round 1

A red job is information, not a failure of the study: the bundle is uploaded with `if: always()`. The corrections are executor, workflow or configuration changes; each is a commit, a new public export and a new round. A further round is needed when a job is red for a defect of the harness, when the implementation hash or a workflow changes (rounds under another implementation hash are not used for the calibration), or when the calibration has fewer than six baseline pairs per view (two rounds of three executions under the final implementation hash). A round is never repeated because a baseline pair was noisy. After the record is written and the thresholds are in the conditions file (which changes its hash, not the implementation hash), one confirming round runs on the non-provisional path; it verifies the path and is not used to adjust a threshold. The calibration record `manifests/e10-calibration-v1.yaml` (record class `E10_CALIBRATION_RECORD`, status LOCKED, the thresholds of each condition, the run ids of the rounds it was computed from) is written only from the baseline pairs of the rounds that the final implementation ran, and its values are stated in two further places, which the measured workflow's gate reads and compares with the record: the locked "Campaign configuration" section of experiments/E10-visual/README.md, as `T_R = <value>` and `T_e = <value>` on the line of each condition, and the conditions file, `conditions[].thresholds` of experiments/E10-visual/conditions-draft.json (null until the calibration; the file is then locked with its sha256). A START, the dispatch authorizations, the frozen prompt, the lock of the configuration and the freeze each need the human research lead's explicit approval.

## 5. Not done by a DRY_RUN

It measures nothing, imports nothing, records no entry in manifests/toolchain-manifest.yaml, changes no expectation, calibrates no threshold from a perturbed capture, and produces no detection rate.
