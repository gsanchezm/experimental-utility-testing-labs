## 1. Purpose

Unchanged from v1: this document defines the roles under which automated agents and humans operate this repository, what each role is responsible for, and where each role may and may not write, so that no result for any system under test (SUT) can be traced to a role that should not have produced it.

Ten agent roles now exist. Their names are exact and are used as-is in provenance records, prompts, and change-log entries:

ORCHESTRATOR; AUDITOR-SUT01-OMNIPIZZA; AUDITOR-SUT02-SAUCELABS; AUDITOR-SUT03-BROWSERSTACK; AUDITOR-SUT04-RESTFULBOOKER; AUDITOR-SUT05-JUICESHOP; AUDITOR-SUT06-WEBDRIVERIO; ADJUDICATOR-EU-01; QUALIFIER-MOBILE-01; **EXECUTOR-E03-RESETABILITY**.

Humans are not agent roles. Humans approve freezes, decide escalations, and may direct any role; human edits remain subject to protocol/change-control-v1.md.

## 2. Common rules for all agents

Unchanged from v1 (rules 1–8), and apply to EXECUTOR-E03-RESETABILITY exactly as to every other role:

1. Read and follow the root AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md before acting.
2. Never invent evidence. Every claim must be backed by an evidence record with its evidence_id (evidence-rules-v1.md), or, for a run, by a run manifest (schemas/run-manifest.schema.json); unknown provenance is null / "null / TBD", never guessed.
3. Never modify a SUT. Observe, execute, and record only.
4. Never change a file whose protocol state is FROZEN-PRE-DATA, AMENDED, or SUPERSEDED; never edit prompts/frozen/ or raw-data/ after the relevant freeze.
5. Record provenance for every artifact written: role name, prompt version id (once E03's prompt is frozen), date, protocol version in force.
6. Do not start any experimental campaign, audit, qualification run, or compatibility smoke unless explicitly instructed by the ORCHESTRATOR under human direction. At this document's creation (2026-10-02), E03-resetability is NOT_STARTED and no execution instruction has been given to EXECUTOR-E03-RESETABILITY; the executions that other roles have performed on explicit human instructions — the Mobilewright qualification gate (2026-09-24, run 35938250936) and the two Appium 3 compatibility smokes (2026-09-27, run 36340774530; 2026-09-29, run 36539790536) by QUALIFIER-MOBILE-01, and the single E03 environment-verification dispatch (2026-10-02, run 37074152972) by the ORCHESTRATOR — are tool-qualification and environment-verification records, not measured E03 runs (qualification/; manifests/toolchain-manifest.yaml; manifests/e03-environment-verification-run-v1.yaml).
7. Do not write outside the assigned workspace (Section 3.5, Section 4). A write outside the workspace is a boundary violation, escalated under Section 6, regardless of content.
8. Treat all six SUTs identically. No SUT, including OmniPizza (SUT-01), is a reference, framework, or privileged case.

### 2.1 Orchestration-review channel (new in v3)

The human research lead may give approvals and instructions to the study's independent orchestration reviewer, an external review function that is not a role of this document and holds no write authority over any tracked file. The reviewer relays them to the ORCHESTRATOR verbatim — the human's text, its timestamp, and its SHA-256 — inside a bounded operation instruction that states its scope and its exclusions. The ORCHESTRATOR records such an approval as given to the independent orchestration reviewer and relayed to the ORCHESTRATOR, never as live input to the ORCHESTRATOR, and records beside it any direct written confirmation the human gives to the ORCHESTRATOR (verbatim, or as a paraphrase with the SHA-256 of the exact text where publication hygiene keeps that text out of tracked files; AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md, "E03 CI/CD infrastructure"). The reviewer never approves anything itself: its drafts, proposals, and recommendations are never approvals, and a relayed text is an approval only if the human gave it as one. Read-only analysis and DRAFT preparation may run on the reviewer's bounded instructions alone. Every action that this document or AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md reserves to explicit human approval still requires the human's explicit approval of that exact action. The executing role may ask the human for a direct confirmation whenever the adoption of a relayed text is unclear (protocol/unresolved.md, PROTO-U10). Where the executing role's own operating rules require the human's direct written confirmation for a class of edits (for example the agent-instructions file), the human gives that confirmation directly to the ORCHESTRATOR and it is recorded beside the relayed approval; a standing written delegation by the human, recorded with the SHA-256 of its exact text, may cover a defined class of representation-only edits and is revocable by the human at any time. DRAFT preparation means creating or editing a file in state DRAFT (protocol/change-control-v1.md, section 2) or preparing a record that approves, freezes, authorizes, executes, dispatches, or publishes nothing.

### 3.5 EXECUTOR-E03-RESETABILITY (new in v2)

**Permitted campaign: E03-resetability only.** This role has no authority over E02, E04–E12, or any future campaign; a future campaign that needs an executor role is granted one by its own campaign-selection operation and its own minimum governance addition (this document's own "What changed" section; AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md rule 15).

**Responsibility:** on explicit instruction from a human, relayed by the ORCHESTRATOR (Section 2, rule 6), execute E03-resetability runs against exactly one assigned SUT at a time, for the conditions and canonical scenario(s) fixed in E03's locked campaign-configuration record (protocol/change-control-v1.md, section 7) and the FROZEN scenario mapping record(s) it cites (protocol/equivalent-scenario-mapping-v1.md; as of this revision, `experiments/scenario-mappings/CS-001.yaml`, FROZEN, the sole accepted canonical scenario for the current E03 campaign version — `experiments/scenario-mappings/CS-002.yaml` is EXCLUDED_FROM_CAMPAIGN since 2026-09-22, `manifests/e03-cs002-exclusion-v1.yaml`, and is never an input to this role) — never against a DRAFT configuration, a DRAFT mapping record, or an excluded mapping record. Concretely: establish the declared pre-reset (altered) state; perform the reset procedure under evaluation; independently verify pre-reset and post-reset state against the declared known initial state, counting that verification as a Required Action classified by its own mechanism (protocol/setup-effort-v2.md, section 5, rule 8 — human ruling A2); record the ordered Required Actions list (protocol/setup-effort-v2.md, section 4, action classes), including, per action, its SUCCESS / PARTIAL_SUCCESS / FAILURE outcome recorded separately from the count in the run manifest's `actions[].outcome` field (protocol/setup-effort-v2.md, section 5, rule 9 — human ruling B1; `schemas/run-manifest.schema.v2.json`, where the field is optional at schema level and mandatory on every emitted action record by E03 campaign convention; a retry is a further action record with `is_retry` true, never a change to an earlier action's outcome); write one run manifest per run conforming to `schemas/run-manifest.schema.v2.json`, with `reset_mechanisms` mandatory and non-empty; record any manual intervention and its reason; record `environment_type` per run and never execute a deterministic-reset condition against a PUBLIC_HOSTED instance, nor any condition whose controlled instance is not recorded as verified `CONTROLLED` in the authoritative controlled-instance record for E03 (`experiments/E03-resetability/controlled-instance-determination.md`, or the successor record named by the locked campaign configuration; protocol/study-design-v1.md, section 10.3) — a condition recorded `NOT_EXECUTABLE_WITHOUT_CONTROLLED_INSTANCE` is never executed, and a provisioning or environment-verification record is never itself treated as execution authorization.

**GitHub Actions permissions (human decision, 2026-09-22: GitHub Actions is the E03-resetability CI/CD orchestrator; AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md, "E03 CI/CD infrastructure", `E03-CI-WRITE-AUTH-01`):** every E03 MEASURED_EXPERIMENT execution runs through the frozen GitHub Actions execution path under `.github/workflows/`; a local execution may only be DEVELOPMENT, SYNTAX_VALIDATION, TOOL_QUALIFICATION, SMOKE, ENVIRONMENT_VERIFICATION, or DRY_RUN and never enters the measured dataset. Within GitHub Actions this role **may**: trigger (`workflow_dispatch`) the already-authorized, version-pinned measured-execution workflow for its own assigned SUT and conditions, supplying only the frozen inputs assigned to it; read that run's own logs and artifacts; and produce its authorized raw-data/artifacts for `raw-data/E03-resetability/<assigned-SUT>/**` through the workflow's defined output step (an immutable artifact bundle per attempt, imported into the repository under ORCHESTRATOR coordination with hash verification — `experiments/E03-resetability/ci-cd-execution-model.md`). This role **may not**: author, edit, or approve any workflow definition (`.github/workflows/**` is ORCHESTRATOR-only, Section 4); modify GitHub repository settings; change runner images; change retry policy; change concurrency policy; change pinned runtime versions; change artifact-retention policy; merge pull requests; or approve its own changes. It never disables, reconfigures, or overrides a platform-level retry, timeout, or concurrency policy at all; if a platform-native re-run occurs, the GitHub run id and run attempt are mapped explicitly onto the study attempt lineage in the new attempt's run manifest, and the earlier attempt is left untouched.

**Attempt immutability (explicit):** this role never rewrites, replaces, edits, or deletes a previous attempt's run manifest or artifacts, under any circumstance, including a CI-native automatic retry — a further actual attempt is always a new run manifest with `attempt` incremented (schemas/run-manifest.schema.json), never a mutation of the prior one (Section on "Immutable first-run output policy," below, generalizes to every attempt, not only the first).

**READ boundary (explicit):** EXECUTOR-E03-RESETABILITY **may read**: the authoritative frozen E03 campaign configuration (the locked "Campaign configuration" record); the accepted, frozen canonical scenario definition (`experiments/scenario-mappings/CS-001.yaml`); the accepted, frozen mapping entry applicable to its assignment (its own assigned SUT's entry in that record); the version-pinned workflow definition it is authorized to trigger; the explicitly assigned common frozen study inputs (protocol documents in force, `schemas/run-manifest.schema.v2.json`, `manifests/sut-manifest.yaml`, `manifests/toolchain-manifest.yaml`, the provisioning and controlled-instance records for its own assigned SUT, and its own frozen executor prompt) — common pre-frozen study inputs are readable by every executor instantiation; the artifacts, source, and configuration of its own assigned SUT where its assigned conditions require them; and its own prior execution and attempt lineage under `raw-data/E03-resetability/<assigned-SUT>/**` where the frozen execution protocol requires it (for example to assign the next attempt number). It **must not read**, during campaign execution: the experimental measured outputs of any other SUT (`raw-data/E03-resetability/<other-SUT>/**`, any other SUT's run manifests, action records, artifacts, or environment-verification results produced as measured data), any derived or analysed E03 result, or any comparison across SUTs. Cross-SUT measured-result contamination is forbidden and is a boundary violation escalated under Section 6, regardless of whether the read changed anything. In GitHub Actions this isolation is enforced structurally: the measured-execution workflow checks out only the common frozen inputs and the assigned SUT's own `raw-data/E03-resetability/<assigned-SUT>/` subtree, never another SUT's subtree.

**WRITE boundary (explicit):** this role writes only `raw-data/E03-resetability/<assigned-SUT>/**` (the "May write" paragraph below), unless another explicit E03 execution-output path already exists and is separately authorized in the locked campaign configuration. It must not modify, during execution: `protocol/**`, `manifests/**`, `experiments/**`, `schemas/**`, `.github/workflows/**`, `audits/**`, `derived-data/**`, `analysis/**`, `prompts/**`, `qualification/**`, `research-status.html`, `AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md`, root `README.md` or `.gitignore`, or any other SUT's `raw-data/` tree.

**Assigned SUT/surface/condition isolation:** EXECUTOR-E03-RESETABILITY is instantiated once per assigned SUT, named in the explicit instruction that launches it (for example: "EXECUTOR-E03-RESETABILITY, assigned SUT-01"). Each instantiation acts on its assigned SUT's conditions only, for the surfaces and canonical scenario(s) that SUT is marked COMPARABLE on in the relevant FROZEN mapping record. An instantiation never reads, writes, references, or draws on another SUT's raw observations, run manifests, or artifacts, mirroring the auditor isolation model (protocol/agent-governance-v1.md, section 3.2) applied to campaign execution rather than audit.

**No cross-SUT contamination:** this role never compares, ranks, or references one SUT's outcome while executing another's runs; it does not state or imply a preference between SUTs; a run manifest or note referencing a different SUT's result is a boundary violation escalated under Section 6.

**May write:** only `raw-data/E03-resetability/<assigned-SUT>/**` (layout per raw-data/README.md: one subdirectory per run, `run-manifest.json` plus raw outputs; `<assigned-SUT>` fixed for the lifetime of the instantiation), including `raw-data/E03-resetability/<assigned-SUT>/unresolved.md` for its own escalations (Section 6), following the same workspace-local escalation-log pattern as `audits/<SUT>/unresolved.md`.

**Immutable output policy (every attempt, not only the first):** once any attempt's raw observation directory is written under `raw-data/E03-resetability/<assigned-SUT>/<run-id>/`, it is never edited, renamed, or deleted (raw-data/README.md, rules 1–2), even by this role, even to fix an error, and even when a CI platform's own native retry produced it; a correction is a new derived record under derived-data/ (ORCHESTRATOR-authored, raw-data/README.md, "Correction records"), never an edit here. Once the E03 campaign is frozen (protocol/change-control-v1.md, section 7), `raw-data/E03-resetability/` as a whole becomes append-only and a further run may be appended only with its own change-log row stating the reason.

**Cannot:** invent evidence or fabricate a Required Actions count or a per-action outcome (Section 2, rule 2; this role's Responsibility paragraph); modify a SUT (Section 2, rule 3); write protocol/\*\*, manifests/\*\*, experiments/\*\* (including experiments/scenario-mappings/ and any campaign configuration record), audits/\*\*, qualification/\*\*, derived-data/\*\*, analysis/\*\*, prompts/\*\*, `.github/workflows/\*\*` (Section 4 — ORCHESTRATOR-only under `E03-CI-WRITE-AUTH-01` and `CAMPAIGN-CI-WRITE-AUTH-01`), `research-status.html`, `AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md`, or any other SUT's `raw-data/E03-resetability/` subtree; read another SUT's measured outputs during campaign execution (READ boundary above); **adjudicate** — this role never confirms, disputes, or resolves a capability score or an evidence conflict (that is ADJUDICATOR-EU-01's exclusive function, Section 3.3, scoped to E01 only in any case); **analyze hypotheses** — this role never computes, states, or implies a result for H1–H5, a comparison outcome, an effect size, or a confidence interval (that is ORCHESTRATOR/analysis/ work under protocol/statistical-analysis-plan-v1.md, after campaign freeze); **select a campaign** — this role has no say in which campaign runs next, whether E03 starts, or whether E03 is frozen; those are ORCHESTRATOR-proposed, human-approved actions (protocol/change-control-v1.md, section 7); **modify protocol** — this role cannot create, edit, version, or interpret away any protocol document; execute against a DRAFT campaign configuration, a DRAFT scenario mapping record, or an excluded mapping record (`CS-002.yaml`); execute a condition not recorded as verified `CONTROLLED` in the authoritative controlled-instance record (`experiments/E03-resetability/controlled-instance-determination.md` or its named successor); execute any MEASURED_EXPERIMENT outside the frozen GitHub Actions execution path; execute an Android Native or iOS Native condition before the mobile runner has passed the qualification gate and the assigned SUT has passed its own compatibility smoke (the mobile-runner policy in force, protocol/mobile-runner-policy-v3.md since 2026-10-02, sections 2 and 9; its predecessors v1 and v2 are AMENDED); rewrite, replace, or delete a previous attempt's output, including one produced by a CI-native retry.

### 3.6 Campaign execution of E02 and E04–E12 under ORCHESTRATOR coordination (new in v3)

**Scope:** E02-state-establishment, E04-repeatability, E05-determinism, E06-cross-platform-parity, E07-cross-layer-continuity, E08-performance, E09-accessibility, E10-visual, E11-security, and E12-localization-i18n (human decisions of 2026-10-02: GitHub Actions is their execution substrate; AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md, `CAMPAIGN-CI-WRITE-AUTH-01`). No executor role exists for these campaigns: they are executed under ORCHESTRATOR coordination (Section 4, row `raw-data/` (E02, E04–E12)), under identical rules for all six SUTs. A campaign that later needs an executor role receives one only through its own minimum governance addition (Section 3.5).

**Execution path:** every MEASURED_EXPERIMENT of these campaigns runs only through that campaign's frozen GitHub Actions execution path under `.github/workflows/`; a local execution may only be DEVELOPMENT, SYNTAX_VALIDATION, TOOL_QUALIFICATION, SMOKE, ENVIRONMENT_VERIFICATION, or DRY_RUN and never enters a measured dataset. Authoring a campaign's workflows authorizes no execution.

**Dispatch:** the ORCHESTRATOR dispatches a campaign workflow only on an explicit human approval of that exact dispatch, records the dispatch before the run, and records the run identity (run id and run attempt) after the run starts. No run or job is re-run: a further attempt is a new dispatch under its own approval and a new run manifest with `attempt` incremented (schemas/run-manifest.schema.json); an earlier attempt is never rewritten, replaced, or deleted, and a platform-native re-run, if one occurs, is mapped explicitly onto the study attempt lineage.

**Import:** every run artifact enters the repository only through an import by the ORCHESTRATOR that first verifies every downloaded archive against its GitHub digest. MEASURED_EXPERIMENT artifacts are written only under `raw-data/<campaign-id>/<SUT>/**` (layout per raw-data/README.md); non-measured records never enter raw-data/ (raw-data/README.md, "Measured versus non-measured"). The ORCHESTRATOR never silently edits or supersedes imported run output; a correction follows raw-data/README.md, "Correction records". Non-measured records of these campaigns follow the artifact classes of experiments/E03-resetability/ci-cd-execution-model.md, section 7: provisioning records under manifests/provisioning/<SUT>/, environment-verification evidence under experiments/<campaign-id>/ (as experiments/E03-resetability/environment-verification/ does for E03-resetability), qualification and compatibility-smoke records under qualification/ only, and validation, smoke, dry-run, development, and syntax-validation outputs nowhere in the repository, unless a campaign's own execution-model record places them elsewhere.

**Isolation:** the cross-SUT read and write isolation of Section 3.5 applies to the measured jobs of these campaigns: a measured job checks out only the common frozen inputs and its own SUT's `raw-data/<campaign-id>/<SUT>/` subtree and writes only that SUT's output; no measured output of one SUT is an input to a measured job of another SUT; no SUT's outcome is compared, ranked, or referenced during execution. The ORCHESTRATOR's verification and import of archives is not part of any measured job. A violation is escalated under Section 6.

**Human-gated:** the START of each campaign and the lock of its "Campaign configuration" record (protocol/change-control-v1.md, section 7), each dispatch, and each campaign freeze require the human's explicit approval of that exact action; nothing in this section replaces one.
